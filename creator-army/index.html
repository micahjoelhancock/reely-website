GOOD QUESTIONS.

Anything else, ask us on the call below.

01What is a Reely army?

The in-house content and creator team you could never afford to hire. 8 to 30+ creators post about your brand every month to their own followers. We do the strategy, recruiting, editing, approvals and reporting. You keep every post.

02Is this for my kind of brand?

If your customers spend time on social media, yes. We recruit creators whose followers match your buyers, whether you sell online, in stores or in person, and we size the army to how many posts you want each month.

03What do I have to do?

Give us one brief: the product, the goal and who you want to reach. We pitch the ideas, then handle recruiting, contracts, disclosure, creator briefs, editing, approvals and reporting.

04What does it cost, all in?

Our fee starts at $3,000 a month, plus a one-time setup from $1,500. Creators earn commission on the sales they bring in, at a rate we agree with you before launch, and nothing upfront. Ad spend is your budget and we take 0% of it. The minimum term is three months.

05Who comes up with the ideas and edits the videos?

We do. Creative strategy and video editing are in the fee on every plan. We pitch the direction to you in the first week.

06Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the army shows up: 20 creator posts live in your first 30 days, or month two is free. Views and sales depend on the product and the market, so we report your real numbers every month.

07How will I know it is working?

You get a monthly report: views, reach, saves and shares, and branded searches, then promo codes per creator and “How did you hear about us?” answers. Army and up adds booking and call tracking.
