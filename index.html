07 / FAQ

Asked often. Answered plainly.

What people ask before they book. Anything else, bring it to the call.

Why not just hire a full-time designer?Q+

Because we’re more affordable, more reliable, and you don’t have to manage us. A senior editor, designer or AI specialist of Reely’s calibre easily costs $100,000 a year per role. With us you get all of that specialisation for one price.

What does “unlimited requests” mean?Q+

Add as many requests to your queue as you like. Your plan sets how many are in production at once.

What if I don’t like the first draft?Q+

Most revisions land the same day or early the next. We’re your ongoing partner, so there’s no limit on drafts.

Can I book a single project?Q+

The monthly membership is the best value by far: pause or cancel when you have what you need. In rare cases we quote a project rate.

What are your turnaround times?Q+

Most requests are delivered in 24-48 hours. Larger pieces, like a packaging system or a site, are scoped with your Creative Director and delivered in stages.

How does the pause feature work?Q+

Have a slow few weeks coming up? Pause and resume as needed. No thirty-day notice, no nonsense.

How do we communicate?Q+

You get a Creative Director for day-to-day contact, a shared Slack channel, and a dashboard to submit requests, track status and download files.

What if I want to cancel?Q+

Membership is month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Cancel anytime.
